About the Studio

Graphic
Design
Studio

Quiet Contrast is a full-service graphic design studio based in Southern California — crafting print, email, web, and brand identities for companies that demand precision and presence.

With over 15 years of experience and a portfolio spanning Tilly's, Brighton, Entrepreneur Magazine, and Burger King, we bring the same exacting standard to every project, regardless of size or industry.

Every project begins with a brief, not a guess. No templates. No filler. Just design that does something.
